1 · Template grouping

Group any screens that feel like the same underlying template or layout skeleton. Ignore color (the sheets are grayscale on purpose) and ignore the choice of typeface unless it is the only difference between two screens. One group per line, screen numbers separated by spaces or commas. A screen with a unique layout is its own group and can be left out; anything you leave out is treated as its own group.

2 · Design quality

Rate each screen 1 to 5. 5 = a designer clearly composed this. 1 = an accident or a broken layout. Consider hierarchy, balance, whitespace, legibility, and whether there is one clear dominant object.
