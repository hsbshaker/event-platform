# DesignIntent — blind review artifact

Each block is one event. It carries the **authoritative creative brief** the three concepts
were generated from, and the **three DesignIntents** that came back, each with the
host-facing `presentation` the concept card would show.

The brief is frozen fixture state written by hand with the case; no model produced it. Only
the three concepts in each block came from a live call. Cite batches and siblings by the
labels used here.

Read the reviewer packet beside this file for what you are being asked.

## Batch 1

**Creative direction**

> This design grounds an emotional family crossroads in shared lineage, memory preservation, and clear spatial accessibility. It balances warm intergenerational storytelling with the solemnity needed for property decisions, without taking sides.

- **Tone keywords:** anchored; contemplative; enduring; tender; intergenerational; solemn
- **Tonal intent:** Warm afternoon daylight filtering through farm oaks shifting into quiet, direct evening light around a shared dining table.
- **Tone explicitly constrained:** no
- **Colors explicitly constrained:** no
- **Required colors:** —
- **Preferred colors:** barnwood gray; faded sage; warm linen; weathered iron; dried clover
- **Avoid colors:** —
- **Dominance notes:** Warm linen and faded sage create a welcoming base for family storytelling, while barnwood gray and weathered iron anchor formal document review sections.
- **Compatible tonal directions:** light; mid
- **Compatible families:** editorial; invitation
- **Compatible typography categories:** oldstyle; soft_serif; transitional
- **Visual motifs:** archival audio waveforms; farm property boundary lines; porch floorboard grain; family memory trees; dried meadow grasses
- **Texture direction:** Sun-washed linen, matte paper stock with slight tooth, and soft press finishes that feel warm to touch and free of harsh glare.
- **Typography direction:** Generous leading and high contrast for effortless legibility across generations, using warm classical serifs for memory collection and sturdy transitional types for legal document review.
- **Copy tone:** Respectful, clear, emotionally grounded, and neutral regarding property outcomes.
- **Host constraints (AUTHORITATIVE):** The eldest uncle relies on a heavy motorized wheelchair, requiring step-free access across the sloped lawn and home.
- **Creative guidance (ADVISORY):** Ensure high contrast and generous font sizing to support older family members during evening document review.; Provide subtle visual separation between narrative memory collection areas and formal real estate deliberation materials.; Maintain a calm, balanced tone that honors family heritage without favoring either selling or keeping the farm.
- **Inspiration:** No visual inspiration supplied.
